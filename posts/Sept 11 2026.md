# Updating the AI

## The Issue
New AI models have been released and I need to keep up with them because they provide models that perform better at the same resource usage. The problem is that I have not updated AI or worked on it for a while, so I need to ensure that things are on track.

## Research
I had to do some research to see:
- What models were now considered the best
- What sizes they were
- What's their TPS
- Are there updates to OpenWebUI
- Does llama.cpp or llama-swap have anything new

## Solution (Model Updates)
I saw that new Qwen Models were released which were the Next Models. These models had 80B with an active 6B. This was quite big for AI in general but for me, I can't reliably fit a model with 80B capabilities in my AI machine. After doing more research, I landed on an older model by Qwen that was the Qwen 3.6 35B A3B. This was a model that was one generation more than the model I use for my general ORION model. I gave it a shot, tested it with different tasks, and it proved to be genuinely useful. It could solve tasks that I considered difficult, and help me with most daily tasks. The major issue that I still have yet to figure out is agentic usage and proper tool usage. None of my models were properly able to have good tool usage and often failed at using them, sometimes not using them at all when it would be far beneficial. An example for this is when I ask it for a fact where it should use web search, but don't explicitly say to search the web, instead I say terms like "research" or "tell me about", it would just recall from its training data. However, even with these issues, the new model upgrade made a significant boost to my stack.
